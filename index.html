VoiceLift gives training suggestions based on your own logged numbers and common strength-training rules of thumb. It is not medical advice and it cannot see your form. If something hurts, stop and talk to a professional.

Estimated 1RM comes from the Epley and Brzycki formulas and is only dependable up to about 10 reps; calories are a MET model based on your bodyweight and time under load, not a measurement.
