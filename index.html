Logging

In hands-free mode nothing is logged until it hears the wake phrase, so talking to someone — or your music — won’t fill the log with nonsense. Say “log it, bench press 185 for 8” in one breath, or say “log it”, wait for the beep, then the set.

Voice log

The last 200 things the microphone heard, what it did with them, and anything you marked wrong. If it is mishearing you, export this and send it — the exact words are the only way to fix it.

Your data
